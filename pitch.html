About LedgerCap

Track your PSX stocks and mutual funds in one place.

A private on-device ledger for Pakistani investors — PSX, Meezan funds, portfolio P&L, and research tools that educate without tipping.

The problem

Holdings scattered. Prices unclear.

  • Broker apps show trades; spreadsheets drift out of date.
  • Funds and stocks live in different places; net worth is a guess.
  • Most tools push advice. People often just want an honest ledger.

The product

What ships today (3.58.11).

  • Hub — net worth, KSE-100 strip, market status, tools grid
  • Markets — PSX stock watch, Meezan funds, watchlist, global & commodities via Worker
  • Portfolio — holdings, transactions, dividends, calendar, Zakat
  • Research — analyzer, screener, signals, paper trade, journal
  • Backup — JSON export, PIN-encrypted .ledgercap.enc, optional Worker cloud sync
