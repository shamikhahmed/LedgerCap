LedgerCap privacy

Effective 25 October 2026 · Version 3.58.11 · Capricorn Systems, Karachi, Pakistan

Summary

LedgerCap works on your device. There are no accounts, analytics SDKs, ads, or trackers. Portfolio holdings stay on your device. Indicative market prices may be fetched through Capricorn’s Cloudflare Worker.

What stays on your device

  • Holdings, watchlists, journals, preferences, and optional Telegram bot token in localStorage / IndexedDB
  • Cached market snapshots for offline display
  • PIN vault material when you enable a security PIN

What may leave your device

ServiceWhat is sentWhyWhen
ledgercap-psx-proxy.shamikhahmed.workers.dev Ticker / market query parameters; normal request metadata Indicative PSX, funds, FX, crypto, news, and snapshot data When you open market screens or refresh prices
GitHub Pages Normal browser request metadata for static app files Host the PWA First visit / service-worker asset refresh
api.telegram.org (optional) Alert text via your own bot token User-configured notifications Only if you enable Telegram and save a token in Settings
TradingView (embedded charts) Chart frame requests per TradingView’s terms Display charts where shown When you open a chart view

Upstream PSX and Yahoo feeds are reached via the Worker only, not from your browser. Prices may be delayed or indicative — nothing here is investment advice.

Your controls

Export or erase portfolio data from Settings. Remove or mask any Telegram token in Settings. Clearing site data deletes local holdings.

Children

LedgerCap is intended for a general audience aged 13 and over.

Disclaimer

LedgerCap is for tracking and education. Prices may be delayed or indicative. Nothing here is investment advice.
