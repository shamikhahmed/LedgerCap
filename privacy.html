LedgerCap privacy

Effective: 14 October 2026 · Published by Capricorn Systems, Karachi, Pakistan

LedgerCap works on your device. We don't have accounts, analytics, ads or tracking, and we don't sell data. Portfolio holdings stay on your device.

What stays on your device

  • Holdings, watchlists, journals, preferences, and optional Telegram bot token in localStorage / IndexedDB.
  • Cached market snapshots for offline display.

What leaves your device

Live and indicative prices use a Capricorn Cloudflare Worker (D-09). The browser does not call Yahoo or PSX directly. Prices may be delayed or indicative — nothing here is investment advice.

ledgercap-psx-proxy.shamikhahmed.workers.dev Ticker / market query parameters; normal request metadata Indicative PSX, funds, FX, crypto, news, and snapshot data When you open market screens or refresh prices

TradingView (embedded charts) Chart frame requests per TradingView’s terms Display charts where shown When you open a chart view

Upstream PSX (dps.psx.com.pk) and Yahoo feeds are reached via the Worker only, not from your browser.

Your controls

Export / erase portfolio data from Settings. Remove or mask any Telegram token in Settings. Clearing site data deletes local holdings.

Children

LedgerCap is intended for a general audience aged 13 and over.
