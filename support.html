Common topics

  • Install on iPhone: Safari → Share → Add to Home Screen.
  • Live prices: Quotes come from Capricorn’s Cloudflare Worker. If unreachable, the app shows cached or fallback data — never silently invents prices.
  • Lost data: Export JSON (or PIN-encrypted backup) from Settings before switching phones. Portfolio is local-only.
  • Demo mode: Open ?demo=1 for a sample portfolio.
  • Languages: English · اردو · Roman Urdu in Settings.
  • Disclaimer: Tracking and education only. Prices may be delayed. Not investment advice.
